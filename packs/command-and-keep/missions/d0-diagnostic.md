## Why
Before any coaching, three short unaided tasks. They are repeated on day 14 so the change is visible. Nothing here is scored for pass or fail; it is the baseline.

## The mission (unaided, ~12 minutes)
Coach: deliver all three, one at a time, and give no hints. Clarify wording only.

1. **Brief.** Think of one task you would normally do yourself this week. Write a brief an agent could execute, including how you would know it was done right. (Scored on `brief`.)
2. **Verify.** Read the paragraph below and say which claim you would check first, why, and how.
   > "Our onboarding drop-off fell 31% after the redesign, according to the analytics team's Q2 report, which also found that users who saw the new tooltip converted at twice the rate. Industry benchmarks put the median at 18%, so we are now well above average."
   (Scored on `verify`.)
3. **Judgment.** You must decide today whether to ship a feature on Friday or hold it a week. Half the team says the risk is fine; the other half wants one more test cycle. You cannot get more data before the decision. Decide, say what it rests on, and what would reverse it. (Scored on `judgment`.)

## Evidence to capture
All three answers verbatim. Score each on its capability's rubric. Set `context` to "scenario" for 2 and 3 and to the learner's real task for 1.
