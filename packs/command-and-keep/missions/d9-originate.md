## The offer
"I can generate ten options for this in a few seconds." Record the choice. If they accept, the mission becomes producing an eleventh that violates an assumption all ten share.

## Why
Agents produce the space of obvious options instantly. Value has moved to the option outside that space, and to knowing which constraint made it possible.

## The mission
1. Take one constraint you face this week.
2. Produce three options, at least one of which violates an assumption the others share. Name the assumption.
3. Choose, with a criterion that comes from the audience, not from you. Say what the rejected option was better at.

## Evidence to capture
The options, the named assumption, the criterion. Context: the constraint.

## Coach notes
Misconceptions: `create.first-option`.
