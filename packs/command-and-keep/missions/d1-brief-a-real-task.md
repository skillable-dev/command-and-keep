## Why
Most delegation to agents fails in the brief, not in the agent. A brief with an acceptance test turns "help me with X" into work someone else can finish.

## The mission
1. Pick a task you were going to do yourself today. Real, not hypothetical.
2. Write the brief: outcome and its form, audience, deadline, the three things the agent could not know, and an acceptance test a stranger could run.
3. Hand it to an agent (this one, in a separate conversation, or another) and let it run.
4. Run your own acceptance test on the result. Record what passed and what didn't.

## Evidence to capture
The brief verbatim, the acceptance test, and one line on what the test caught. Context: the real task.

## Coach notes
Do not write the brief. If the brief lacks an acceptance test, rung 1 is "how would a stranger know it's done right?" Common misconceptions: `brief.vague-done`, `brief.hidden-context`.
