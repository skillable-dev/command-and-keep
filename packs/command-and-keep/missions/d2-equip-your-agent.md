## Why
A skill file is the difference between an agent that talks about your job and one that does a piece of it your way. The skill is where your judgement lives once it leaves your head.

## The mission
1. Choose a procedure you do weekly with at least three decisions inside it.
2. Write a SKILL.md: when to use it, then the decisions as "when X, do Y, because Z" lines, then what it must refuse or escalate.
3. Run it on three real cases (or the fallback scenario). Note every deviation from what you would have done.
4. Fix each deviation in the skill, not in the output. Re-run one case.

## Evidence to capture
The skill file (final), the list of deviations, and which one surprised you. Context: the procedure.

## Coach notes
Do not draft the skill. If they describe the job in prose, rung 1 is "where are the decisions?" Misconceptions: `equip.procedure-as-prose`, `equip.fix-the-output`.
