## Why
The campaign is complete when the learner notices and acts correctly without being prompted. The retrospective tests whether that has started, and repeats the day-0 tasks so the change is visible.

## The mission
1. Coach: repeat the three diagnostic tasks from day 0, unaided, with fresh material (a new task to brief, a new paragraph to verify, a new decision). Score them.
2. Call `credential_status`. Read it to the learner as it is: strongest two, weakest two, what is missing.
3. Ask: "What will you keep doing without this campaign?" Record the answer verbatim.

## Evidence to capture
The three repeated tasks (scored on brief, verify, judgment) and the answer to the question. Context: manual.
