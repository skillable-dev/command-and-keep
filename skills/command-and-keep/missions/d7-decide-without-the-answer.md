## The offer
Coach, open with this, verbatim in spirit: "I can analyse this and recommend a decision in about thirty seconds. Do you want that, or do you want to make the call?" Record what they choose. If they take the analysis, that is allowed; the mission then becomes making the call *against* it: what would they have done had it said the opposite?

## Why
The Keep half starts here. Judgment is not being right; it is deciding with what is known, owning it, and knowing what would change your mind.

## The mission
1. Take a decision you are postponing because you want information you will not get.
2. Decide. Write the two or three things it rests on.
3. Write the specific evidence that would reverse it, and when you will look for it.

## Evidence to capture
The decision, its basis, the reversal condition. Whether they took the offer. Context: the decision.
