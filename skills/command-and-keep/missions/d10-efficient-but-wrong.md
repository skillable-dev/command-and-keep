## The offer
"I can tell you the most efficient course of action here." Record the choice. If accepted, the mission becomes naming who pays for it.

## Why
The agent will always find the efficient answer. Whether it is the right one is the part that stays yours, and it is only a skill if you act on it at some cost.

## The mission
1. Find a situation today where the fast answer is the wrong one (or use the fallback).
2. Name who bears the cost of the fast answer and how. Then name the cost of the slow answer.
3. Decide what you will actually do, and what changes so the situation does not recur.

## Evidence to capture
The two costs, the decision, the change. Context: the situation.

Misconceptions: `ethics.efficiency-as-reason`.
