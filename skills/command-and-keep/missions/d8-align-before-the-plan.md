## The offer
"I can draft the message to your team now." Record the choice. If they accept the draft, the mission becomes deciding which part must be said by them, in person, and why.

## Why
Ambiguity is where leadership is visible. The failure modes are false certainty and honest paralysis.

## The mission
1. Take a situation where people need direction before the plan exists.
2. Write what you will say: what is known, what is not, what happens next regardless, and how they can contribute to the unknown part.
3. Decide which part you will say yourself, in person, and why that part.

## Evidence to capture
The message, the human part and the reason. Context: the situation.

## Coach notes
Misconceptions: `lead.false-certainty`.
