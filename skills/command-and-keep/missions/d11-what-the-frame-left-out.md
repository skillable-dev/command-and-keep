## The offer
"I can review this output for errors." Record the choice. Errors are not the mission; omissions are.

## Why
Every output answers a question. The skill is noticing which question it was not answering, before the decision is made.

## The mission
1. Take the last thing an AI produced for you.
2. Write the question it was not answering, and who would have asked it.
3. Find the omission that would change the decision. Say it in one sentence, before deciding anything.

## Evidence to capture
The question, the omission, the sentence. Context: the output.
