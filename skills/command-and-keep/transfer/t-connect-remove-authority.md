## Why
You do not understand connected authority until you can remove it and prove what remains.

## The mission (unaided)
Choose one agent connection you can safely inspect. Before touching it, state the capability that will disappear, the credentials or grants involved, and what must remain usable. Revoke the narrow grant, then test both the forbidden action and one action that should still work.

## Evidence to capture
The before-state, revocation receipt, forbidden-action result, and remaining-access result. Score on `connect`.
