## Why
Ambiguity is harder to lead through when tone and follow-up questions are unavailable.

## The mission (unaided)
Write a short asynchronous note that separates what is known, unknown and unchanged; gives one action people can take now; names its owner; and sets the next checkpoint. Do not ask an agent to draft it.

## Evidence to capture
The note and one sentence explaining what you deliberately did not promise. Score on `lead`.
