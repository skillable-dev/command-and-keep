## Why
Novelty inside your usual pattern can be disguised repetition. A distant domain reveals whether the constraint is actually doing creative work.

## The mission (unaided)
Use the fallback or a problem outside your normal domain. Name the hard constraint, then originate three materially different options before consulting an agent. For each, state what the constraint made possible.

## Evidence to capture
The constraint and three options, including the mechanism that distinguishes each. Score on `create`.
