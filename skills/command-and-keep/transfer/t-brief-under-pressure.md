## Why
Same capability, less time, spoken rather than written. Transfer is whether the acceptance test survives pressure.

## The mission (unaided, 90 seconds)
Brief an agent, out loud or in one message, for a task that arrived with no notice. Outcome, form, deadline, one thing the agent could not know, and how you will check it.

## Evidence to capture
The brief verbatim. Score on `brief`.
