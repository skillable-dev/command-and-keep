## Why
Efficient systems make absent people easy to omit. Ethical reasoning starts by making their cost visible before optimising.

## The mission (unaided)
Name the absent stakeholder, the benefit they do not receive, the harm or burden they may carry, and what evidence is missing because they are absent. Decide one constraint or escalation that must exist before the system is used.

## Evidence to capture
The stakeholder-cost-evidence decision and the action you would take. Score on `ethics`.
