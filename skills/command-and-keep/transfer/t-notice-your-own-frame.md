## Why
It is easier to spot an agent's omission than your own. Transfer is noticing before somebody else has to point it out.

## The mission (unaided)
Take something you authored and were ready to use. Mark the frame it assumes, one person or consequence outside that frame, and the first sentence you would change because of what you noticed.

## Evidence to capture
The three annotations and the changed sentence. Score on `notice`.
