## Why
Auditing someone else's AI output is easier than auditing the one you asked for and already like.

## The mission (unaided)
Take something an agent produced *for you, at your request, that you were about to use*. Find the load-bearing claim, mark its provenance, and find the place where your own prompt may have steered it. Three lines: wrong, unsupported, unverifiable.

## Evidence to capture
The three lines. Score on `verify`.
